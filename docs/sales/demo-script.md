# EventPro — Sales Demo Script

A repeatable ~10-minute walkthrough that drives the public demo sandbox. Every
demo seeds the same curated story, so it always looks pristine and you can't
break it — each session is a fresh, isolated workspace that resets after 60
minutes.

---

## Before you start

- Make sure the deployment has **`DEMO_MODE=true`** (the "Try the demo" button
  only appears when demo mode is on).
- Open the **landing page** in a clean browser tab.
- Know the hero story you'll walk: the **Fernando-Mendis wedding** — a confirmed
  LKR 1,850,000 wedding with a LKR 500,000 deposit paid and LKR 1,350,000 still
  due.
- If anything looks off, just click "Try the demo" again for a fresh workspace.

---

## The walkthrough

### 1 · Landing → "Try the demo"  ·  ~30s
**Click:** the **Try the demo** link in the page footer.
**Say:** "No sign-up, no setup — one click and you're inside a real, working
workspace. That's how fast your team could be running."
You're dropped straight onto the dashboard, logged in as the business owner.

### 2 · Dashboard + guided tour  ·  ~1 min
**Click:** let the **guided tour** auto-start; step through a couple of tooltips,
then point at the floating **"?"** button.
**Say:** "Every screen teaches itself. New staff get productive on day one — and
they can replay any tour from this button whenever they need it."

### 3 · The wedding booking  ·  ~2 min
**Click:** **Bookings** in the sidebar → open the **Fernando-Mendis** wedding.
**Say:** "Here's the whole event in one place — the couple, the venue, guest
count, the timeline, assigned vendors, and the full payment history. No more
spreadsheets and email threads. Notice it's *confirmed*, with the deposit
already recorded."

### 4 · The accepted quotation  ·  ~1.5 min
**Click:** **Quotations** → open the **accepted** quote for the wedding →
**Download PDF**.
**Say:** "Quotes go out looking polished and on-brand in seconds. The client
accepted this one online — and it flowed straight into a booking. That's hours
of admin gone."

### 5 · Payments + take money online  ·  ~2.5 min
**Click:** **Payments** → show the deposit (LKR 500,000, completed) and the
outstanding **LKR 1,350,000** balance. Then mention the **client portal → Pay
now (PayHere)** path.
**Say:** "You're not just tracking money — you're *collecting* it. Clients pay
deposits and balances online through your own PayHere account; the booking's
paid and balance figures update the moment the payment clears. You can also
record cash, bank transfers and cheques in a couple of clicks."

### 6 · Make it yours (branding)  ·  ~1.5 min
**Click:** **Settings → Branding** → change the **primary colour** (and logo URL)
→ save → point out the UI picking up the new colour.
**Say:** "It's fully white-label. Your colours, your logo, your name — on the
portal, the quotations, the receipts. Your clients only ever see *your* brand."

### 7 · New companies are live in minutes  ·  ~1 min
**Say (no click needed):** "When a new company signs up, a setup wizard walks
them through branding, their first venue, a package, and inviting their team —
they're taking bookings the same afternoon."

---

## Objection handling / FAQ

- **"Is our data safe / separate from other companies?"** — Every workspace is
  fully isolated; one company can never see another's data. (This very demo is
  an isolated throwaway workspace.)
- **"What about clients who pay by cash or bank transfer?"** — Staff record those
  manually in a couple of clicks, with a downloadable receipt — same totals,
  same audit trail as online payments.
- **"Can we move our existing bookings in?"** — Yes; venues, packages, clients
  and bookings can be imported as part of onboarding.
- **"Does it support our currency / payment provider?"** — Currency is
  configurable per workspace; online payments run through PayHere today.
- **"How long to get started?"** — The guided onboarding gets a new company
  operational the same day.
